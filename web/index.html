Scroll through the night

While you slept, we read 13 newsrooms.

  1. Every outlet files its own version. Dawn, Geo, BBC Urdu and the rest, in English and Urdu. We read each feed’s headline and description, never the article.
  2. We group the versions of each event. Nineteen headlines, five events, across English and Urdu, by a model on our own server.
  3. The label is the count. Five outlets reported it, so it says “Reported by 5 outlets”. One outlet, and it says one. A count, never a verdict.

Then we pick ten, by a formula you can read.
